👋 ABOUT ME

I'm Lewi Ephrem.

01 — WHO I AM

A curious developer who enjoys building things.

Hi, I'm Lewi Ephrem. I'm a student and developer interested in web development, programming, UI/UX design, and technology.

I enjoy taking ideas and turning them into real projects that I can see, test, improve, and actually use.

Most of the projects I work on start with curiosity. I see something interesting and think:

"What if I tried to build this?"

That curiosity motivates me to keep learning and experimenting with new ideas.

02 — WHAT I ENJOY

Things I enjoy doing.

💻

Building Websites

I enjoy creating websites and interfaces and watching them develop from simple ideas into complete projects.

🎨

UI & UX

I like experimenting with layouts, colors, animations, glass effects and modern interfaces.

🧠

Problem Solving

When something doesn't work, I enjoy finding the problem and figuring out how to fix it.

🔧

Experimenting

I like trying new technologies and combining different ideas to create interesting projects.

03 — HOW I LEARN

I learn by building.

I learn best when I can take an idea and actually build something with it.

Instead of only studying tutorials, I like starting with a project and learning what I need along the way.

Sometimes things work immediately. Sometimes they don't.

When something breaks, I try to understand why it happened and how I can fix it.

Those problems often teach me more than simply following a tutorial.

04 — MY MINDSET

Code is only part of the process.

I believe good development isn't only about writing code.

It's also about understanding the problem, thinking creatively and considering the person who will actually use the application.

That's one of the reasons I'm interested in both development and UI/UX.

💡

Build things.
Break things.
Learn from them.
Build again.

05 — WHAT I'M WORKING TOWARD

I'm just getting started.

My goal is to continue improving as a developer and eventually become capable of building larger and more advanced applications from start to finish.

I want to continue improving my programming, web development, UI/UX, databases, backend development and other areas of technology.

Most importantly, I want to keep building projects that aren't only interesting to create,but can also be useful to real people.
